Coloured by time, not by stain.

Oak that lay for millennia under water and peat, sealed from oxygen. Tannins reacted with minerals and darkened the wood all the way through.

  1. Tannins meet mineralsIn oxygen-free water, tannins in the wood react with minerals and slowly darken it.
  2. No two logs are alikeSome are honey-brown, some nearly black. The water decided, not us.
  3. Raised, not felledThe trees were never cut down. Each trunk is located by hand and lifted.
  4. DatedA sample from the trunk is carbon-dated, so the age is known, not guessed.

  2. IIUmber
