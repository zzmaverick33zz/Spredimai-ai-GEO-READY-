# Stats Section with Odometer Numbers

> Stats section that puts one number on stage at a time: a bold colour field, one huge condensed figure that rolls in like an odometer, the line that explains it, a short story in the corner and the key word set huge along the bottom. Step with arrows, keys, wheel or click. For about pages, agency home pages and impact reports.
